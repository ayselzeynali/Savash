# Savash — Buyer Overview

## Short description
Savash is a mobile-friendly, top-down squad-combat game. The player leads a five-soldier squad through a 30-wave campaign, fights increasingly difficult enemy groups, and faces boss encounters every fifth wave.

## Included gameplay
- 30 campaign waves.
- Boss encounters on waves 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30.
- Five-soldier squad with automatic support fire.
- Touch movement controls plus keyboard controls.
- Hold-to-fire combat, pause/resume, and a main menu.
- Built-in soundtrack, music toggle, volume control, and optional user-selected audio.

## Technical delivery
- Android application ID: `com.savash.game`
- HTML/JavaScript game source and local media assets.
- Capacitor 7 Android wrapper.
- GitHub Actions workflows for Android APK builds and source handover ZIP.
- Source archive excludes the separate `TABRIZ-WORLD` project.

## What is not represented as included
Unless expressly added to a signed sale agreement, this overview does not promise:
- Google Play, Cafe Bazaar, or other store publication or approval.
- Store developer accounts, hosting, domain names, or third-party accounts.
- Transfer of private signing keys or passwords.
- Ongoing maintenance, updates, localization, multiplayer, cloud saves, analytics, or monetization systems.
- Legal clearance of third-party media or software licenses.
